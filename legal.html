Privacy Policy: PDF Maestro App

Last updated: December 22, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the PDF Maestro app (hereinafter referred to as 'the Application') created by the developer (hereinafter referred to as 'the Service Provider') as a Free service. This service is intended for use 'AS IS'.

By downloading or using the Application, you are consenting to the terms of this policy. If you do not agree with the terms, please do not use the Application.

2. Information Collection and Use

The Service Provider is committed to protecting your privacy. Unlike many other applications, PDF Maestro operates completely offline and does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

To provide a clear understanding of our data practices:

  • Personal Data: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • Usage Data: We do not track how you use the app or which files you open.
  • Device Information: We do not collect your device ID, IP address, or advertising ID.

3. File Access and Permissions

To function as a PDF reader, the Application requires specific permissions to access files on your device.

Storage Permission (Read/Write): The Application requests access to your device's storage. This permission is strictly used for:

  • Listing PDF files available on your device so you can select them.
  • Opening and rendering the PDF files you choose.
  • Saving any edits or changes locally to your device (if applicable).

Important: The Application processes all files locally on your device. No files, documents, or content are ever uploaded to a cloud server or shared with third parties.

4. Cookies

Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. These are sent to your browser from the websites that you visit and are stored on your device's internal memory.

Since the Application operates offline and does not connect to web services, this Service does not use 'cookies'.

5. Service Providers

The Service Provider does not employ third-party companies or individuals due to the offline nature of the Application. Therefore, no third party has access to your personal information or your files through this Application.

6. Security

The Service Provider values your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, since no data is transmitted over the internet by the Application, the security of your data is primarily dependent on the security of your own device.

7. Links to Other Sites

The Application itself does not contain links to external websites. However, PDF documents you open may contain hyperlinks embedded by their authors. If you click on a third-party link within a PDF, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by the Service Provider. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of these websites. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. In the case strictly hypothetically that we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we would immediately delete this from our records. However, since the app does not collect data, it is inherently safe for children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

10. Contact Us
